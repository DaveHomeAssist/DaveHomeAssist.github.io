Try a rehearsal

In Sessions, choose Open sample rehearsal. Open the two passes of cue 12.5 in Repeats and choose +0.75 s. Compare the color onstage, then attach a note to the photo you want the team to use.

Record a camera without a console

In Settings, choose a camera. Under MIDI, select Record without MIDI. Start a session and choose where to save it. Once recording starts, check that the received and written frame counts advance. Open the finished session from Sessions to review it by time.

Check a network camera

Ask the camera operator for a read-only RTSP stream that supports an additional viewer. Enter its address when you start. The recorder uses TCP and accepts a stream without embedded credentials. In Health, open Video to check for the first decoded frame. If a feed drops, the cue log can continue. Stop the session and verify the movie before starting a new run.

Recover a review

Use Browse for capture folder in Sessions to open the original Capture folder. If macOS asks for the containing folder when you save a note, select the folder containing the capture. Notes are saved beside the capture. Check for the saved confirmation, then export notes as CSV when you need to share them.

For help, email avbydave@gmail.com with your macOS version, the camera source you chose and what you saw.

Privacy policy

Rehearsal Capture records on your Mac. The app saves the silent movie, timed stills, cue events and recording diagnostics in the destination you choose. Review notes and verdicts are stored alongside the capture. You control who can access that location and any copies or exports you share.

The app uses the camera or QuickTime window you select for a recording. For a network camera, it connects to the stream address you enter. Optional local OSC input listens on this Mac. The app runs without an account. It has no advertising or analytics service and does not send rehearsal footage, cue logs or notes to the developer.

macOS stores your app preferences and selected folder permissions locally. You can change camera, local network and screen recording permissions in System Settings. To remove a rehearsal, use Finder to remove its capture folder, review files and any exported copies you control. If you keep them on a shared or cloud-synced drive, that service controls its own copies.

If you email support, the developer receives the message and attachments you choose to send. For a question about privacy or to request removal of a support message, email avbydave@gmail.com.
